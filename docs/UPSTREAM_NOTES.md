# Upstream feature and licensing notes

Freq Music is an original client implementation informed by the public feature sets of Echo Music, BitChord, and SpotiFLAC Extension. Echo Music and BitChord are GPL-3.0 projects; SpotiFLAC Extension is Apache-2.0. This repository does not copy their branding, screenshots, proprietary service credentials, or copyrighted music.

The app may implement compatible user-facing concepts such as search, queue, lyrics, Canvas-style visuals, lossless quality selection, extensions, and Listen Together. YouTube Music and JioSaavn connections must use user-authorized, policy-compliant APIs or extension adapters; the app must not bypass DRM, scrape protected streams, or redistribute copyrighted audio.
